## Assignment Description 

This program is designed to play a song via the Arduino built-in buzzer when a switch is turned on. When the swtich is turned off, the music stops playing when it finishes its loop.

#### Tip for Past Self...

I would remind my past self to make the note delays constants in the first place. At first I had them as their raw values, but it is definitely clearer to make them constants -- which I did! It takes away the extra step of remembering which value belongs to which type of note.
