
# Ghost Detector

Laurel Barrett

## Assignment Description 

The purpose of this assignment is to create a ghost detector. Using a light sensor and a temperature sensor, two red LED pins on the Arduino will light up when it is cold enough and dark enough -- signaling a ghost was detected. The threshholds are determined by the temperature and light value of the specific room you are testing.
